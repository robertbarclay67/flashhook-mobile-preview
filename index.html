Before you jump in

A few things to know.

What can I try right now?

The early studio preview includes the four studio modes, local idea saving, experimental microphone capture, theme settings, and local storyboard tools. Provider connections and AI generation are not active.

Can I use it on my computer or phone?

Flash-Hook is being built for modern browsers on Windows, Android, macOS, iPhone/iPad, and Linux. Device testing is ongoing; microphone recording, playback, and downloads can vary by browser.

Where are my ideas and recordings saved?

Today’s preview stores data in this browser on this device. It does not sync or back up to an account. Download important recordings before clearing browser data. Data from the earlier github.io address does not automatically move to this new domain.

Can I register or subscribe now?

Not yet. Registration, login, and paid plans are coming soon. There is no live checkout and this page does not collect account credentials or card details.
