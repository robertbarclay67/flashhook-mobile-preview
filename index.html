Before you jump in

A few things to know.

What can I try right now?

The early studio preview includes the four studio modes, local idea saving, experimental microphone capture, theme settings, and local storyboard tools, a visual Music beat sequencer, file imports, and optional private Idea Pool sync. Provider connections and AI generation are not active.

Can I use it on my computer or phone?

Flash-Hook is being built for modern browsers on Windows, Android, macOS, iPhone/iPad, and Linux. Device testing is ongoing; microphone recording, playback, and downloads can vary by browser.

Where are my ideas and recordings saved?

Ideas, recordings, and imported files save in this browser first. Log in and choose “Sync this device” in the app or Quick Capture to copy them to your private account. Use the same account on your phone and computer. Keep downloads as backups, and avoid enabling sync on shared devices. Data from the earlier github.io address does not automatically move to this new domain.

Can I register or subscribe now?

You can register for a free account and log in. Paid plans and checkout are coming soon, so no subscription starts when you register. Your password is handled by our secure account service, Supabase; payment details are not collected.
